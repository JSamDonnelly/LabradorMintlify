# Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 27th, 2026

## Introduction

Labrador Accessibility Inc. is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our accessibility testing platform.

### Personal Information

- Email address (for account creation and communication)
- Name (if provided)
- Payment information (processed securely through Stripe)

### Usage Information

- Audit data and test results you create
- Project information and page URLs you test
- Screenshots and files you upload
- Usage patterns and feature interactions

We process your personal information based on the following legal grounds:

### Contract Performance

- Provide and maintain our accessibility testing services
- Process payments and manage subscriptions
- Provide customer support

### Legitimate Interest

- Improve our platform and develop new features
- Analyze usage patterns to enhance user experience
- Maintain platform security and prevent fraud

### Legal Obligation

- Comply with applicable laws and regulations
- Respond to legal requests and prevent illegal activities

### Consent

- Send marketing communications (where explicitly opted in)
- Use non-essential cookies or tracking (where applicable)

## Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. However, we do share your information with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate and improve our platform:

### Third-Party Services

The services below handle personal information. For the complete, current list of every provider that processes any of your data — including our database, file storage, and AI provider — see [Your Data](https://www.testwithlabrador.com/trust).

- **Stripe:** Payment processing and subscription management (processes payment information securely)

- **Google Analytics:** Website analytics (receives anonymized usage data)
- **LinkedIn Insight Tag:** Advertising analytics and retargeting (receives page visit and conversion data to measure ad performance)
- **Mailmodo:** Email delivery service (receives email addresses for account notifications and invitations)

These service providers are bound by strict confidentiality agreements and data processing agreements. They are only permitted to use your information to provide services to us and are prohibited from using it for their own purposes.

### Other Sharing Circumstances

- With your explicit consent for specific purposes
- When required by law or legal process
- To protect our rights, privacy, safety, or property
- In connection with a business transaction (merger, acquisition, or sale of assets)

## Data Security

We implement security measures to protect your data. Our platform uses multiple layers of protection to help keep your information secure.

### Security Headers

- **HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS)** - Enforces HTTPS connections
- **Cross-Origin Resource Policy (CORP)** - Restricts cross-origin resource access
- **Referrer Policy** - Controls referrer information sharing
- **X-Frame-Options & Frame Ancestors** - Prevents clickjacking attacks
- **X-Content-Type-Options** - Prevents MIME type sniffing
- **Content Security Policy (CSP)** - Controls which resources can be loaded

### Additional Protections

- All data transmitted over HTTPS (encrypted in transit)
- Rate limiting on API endpoints to prevent abuse
- Input validation on all form submissions
- Secure password hashing using bcrypt
- Payment information processed securely through Stripe (we do not store card details)

## Your Rights (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other applicable data protection laws, you have the following rights:

- **Right of Access:** Request copies of your personal information
- **Right to Rectification:** Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Right to Erasure:** Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal obligations)
- **Right to Data Portability:** Export your data in a structured, machine-readable format
- **Right to Object:** Object to processing based on legitimate interests
- **Right to Restrict Processing:** Limit how we process your data in certain circumstances
- **Right to Withdraw Consent:** Withdraw consent for processing based on consent
- **Right to Opt Out:** Unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at [help@testwithlabrador.com](mailto:help@testwithlabrador.com). We will respond to your request within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority if you believe we have not handled your personal data appropriately.

## Data Retention

We retain your personal information for different periods depending on the type of data and our legal obligations:

- **Account Information:** Retained while your account is active and for 30 days after account deletion
- **Audit Data & Projects:** Retained while your account is active and for 90 days after account deletion for backup purposes
- **Payment Information:** Processed by Stripe according to their retention policies and legal requirements (typically 7 years for tax purposes)
- **Support Communications:** Retained for 2 years to improve customer service
- **Security Logs:** Retained for 1 year for security and fraud prevention

We may retain some information longer where required by law (such as financial records for tax purposes). When personal information is no longer needed, we securely delete it from our systems.
