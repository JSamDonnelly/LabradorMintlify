# Accessibility

At Labrador we believe everyone deserves equal access to our services. We're actively working to make our website welcoming and usable for all visitors, including people with disabilities.

## Voluntary Product Accessibility Template (VPAT)

For detailed information about our website's conformance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) version 2.2 level AA, please review our most recent VPAT:

[Download VPAT (.DOCX)](https://www.testwithlabrador.com/Labrador_VPAT.docx)

## Our Commitment to Accessibility

We're working to meet WCAG 2.2 at Level AA conformance. These guidelines help us create a better experience for people using screen readers, keyboard navigation, and other assistive technologies.

We conduct regular accessibility audits using both automated tools and manual testing to identify and address barriers. While we strive to maintain WCAG 2.2 Level AA conformance, new accessibility issues can emerge as we update and add content to our website.

## Third-Party Components

Our website uses Intercom for live chat support. We are aware that Intercom has some accessibility limitations, including issues with screen reader announcements and focus management.

Accessible Alternatives:

If you experience difficulty using our chat feature, you can reach us through these channels where we also respond quickly:

- Email: [help@testwithlabrador.com](mailto:help@testwithlabrador.com)
- [Visit support form](https://www.testwithlabrador.com/support)

## We Want to Hear From You

Your feedback helps us improve. If you encounter any accessibility issues while using our website, please email us at [help@testwithlabrador.com](mailto:help@testwithlabrador.com).

We'll do our best to respond within 1 business day and work with you to find a solution.
